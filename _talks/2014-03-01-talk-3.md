In May 2024, I participated in the [Third Serbian International Conference on Applied Artificial Intelligence (SICAAI)](http://aai2024.kg.ac.rs/](http://aai2024.kg.ac.rs/), a conference in Kragujevac, Serbia. I presented my work in bioinformatics, titled "Exploring Word2Vec Models for Capturing the Similarity of Codon Embeddings."
In my session, I received the **award for the best student work**.
